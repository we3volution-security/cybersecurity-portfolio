## Two strands, one direction

**Learning security.** Structured hands-on training through TryHackMe, backed by formal study (NCFE Level 3) and industry certification prep (Security+). The [labs](labs/) record what I actually did at a terminal — the commands, the environment and what I understood afterwards — not room completions.

**Applying it.** I design and run a public education platform covering wallet security, phishing, social engineering and scam recognition. Researching those topics well enough to teach them to non-technical people is a real driver of my own learning, and it forces me to be accurate rather than approximate.

The two connect directly: the [Stay Safe hub](https://we3volution.com/stay-safe) exists because attacks mostly succeed through people, and understanding the technical mechanism is what lets me explain the human one honestly.
